The Story

What's Going On?

🐉

A little dragon needs our help

A friendly baby cloud-dragon named Pip got blown out of the sky in a big windstorm.

Now she's lost on the ground and misses her family up in the Cloud Castle.

Over four little adventures, our heroes help Pip travel through the forest and climb all the way back home! ☁️

How to play

The Super-Simple Rules

🎲 Roll the dice to see if things work!

1–2
Oops! Let's try a different way.
3–4
You did it — it was a little tricky!
5–6
WOW! You did it super easily!
🛟 The Safety Net: nobody can lose. A low roll just means try again or a friend helps — after two tries, it always works!

Session 1

The Crying Cloud

☁️

Hook · In sunny Mossy Meadow the heroes hear a tiny sniffle — it's Pip, lost and far from home!

Comfort Pip — a hug, a song, a silly dance, or a snack? Roll the d6!

Find Pip's floaty cloud-feather hidden in the meadow (count the feathers — which one floats up?).

✅ Win · Pip stops crying and the floaty feather points the way onward.

🛟 If a roll is low, a friendly critter waddles over and points right at it. Zero prep needed!

7
Session 2

The Giggle River

🌊

Hook · The feather leads to the Tickle River — too wide to step across. How will everyone get over?

Cross it: build a log bridge, hop the stepping-stones (count them!), or Rumble drives across.

Cheer up the splashy, giggly fish (Fizzle's happy rainbow fire?) so they form a fishy bridge.

✅ Win · Everyone reaches Tall-Tree Hill, laughing and a little splashed.

🛟 Fall in? The water is warm and knee-deep — the fish nudge you safely to shore.

8
Session 3

The Goblin King's Star

👑

Hook · The Sky Stairs need a fallen star to wake up — but the Goblin King keeps it in his cozy cave.

Make a grumpy friend: make him laugh, play a game, or give a gift. He's just lonely!

Once he smiles he gives you the star. Place it on the stairs and count them glowing (1–5).

✅ Win · A new friend made, the star handed over, and the Sky Stairs light up!

🛟 He's never scary — just grouchy. After two tries his frown always cracks into a smile.

9
Session 4

Up to the Cloud Castle

🏰

Hook · The big climb — up the Sky Stairs, through the clouds, all the way home!

Cross the bouncy cloud-bridge: Rumble jumps it, Bolt ramps over, or bounce across together.

Find Pip's family — a gentle "guess & match" from her clues ("purple wings, swirly tail!").

✅ Win · Pip leaps into her family's wings — a big rainbow party in the sky! 🎉

🛟 There's no wrong family to pick — Pip's mama always knows her baby.

For the kids

How Rolling Works

🎯

It's just like TAG!

Every creature has a Tag Number. Roll your big dice and try to get that number or higher.

Bolt wants to tag a silly Slime (Tag Number 10). He rolls a 14 — that's bigger… TAG! The Slime giggles and sits down. 🟢

A tiny roll? The Slime wiggles away — so just try again!

12
For the grown-up

You've Got This!

🎭
Use silly voices. Let your kid be the hero of the story.
💛
No failing — ever. A low roll is a funny moment, not a dead end.
😟
If they look worried, dial it down: clouds get fluffier, creatures get friendlier.
🎉
Celebrate every single roll!
🎲
Start with a few d6. Add the shiny d20 once they love rolling.
