For the Grown-Up

Game Leader Cheat Sheet

The only rule you need

Roll 1 d6 when a hero tries something tricky:
1-2
"Oops! Let's try a different way."
3-4
"You did it — it was a bit tricky!"
5-6
"WOW — you did it easily!"

🛟 Safety net: nobody can lose. A low roll = try again or a friend helps. After two tries, it just works.

Golden rules

🎭 Silly voices. Let your kid be the hero.

💛 Say "yes, and…" to whatever they imagine.

🎉 Celebrate every roll — even the low ones (they're funny!).

😟 If they look worried, make everything fluffier & friendlier.

🤔 Stuck? Just say one of these:

"What do you want to do?" — let them decide.

"Want to roll the dice to try?" — turns any idea into a fun moment.

Offer choices: "You could climb it, go around it, or… something else?"

"What does your hero see / hear / smell?" — pull them into the world.
